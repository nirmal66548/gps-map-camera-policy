# Privacy Policy for GPS Map Camera

**Effective Date:** November 8, 2026

**GPS Map Camera** ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our app.

## 1. Permissions We Request & How We Use Them
To provide live location stamping on photos and videos, our app requests the following permissions:
* **Camera (`android.permission.CAMERA`):** Used strictly to preview and capture photos and videos within the app. All captured media is processed and stored locally on your device.
* **Microphone (`android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO`):** Used solely to record audio when you capture videos in Video Mode.
* **Location (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` & `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`):** Used to fetch your real-time GPS coordinates, altitude, speed, and address to stamp onto your photos and videos.
* **Internet (`android.permission.INTERNET`):** Used to fetch map tiles, reverse-geocoded street addresses, and live weather information for your current coordinates.

## 2. Data Collection and Storage
* **Zero Cloud Storage of Media:** All photos, videos, custom notes, and project tags captured using GPS Map Camera are stored **locally on your device** (inside your device's internal database and gallery/MediaStore). We do not upload, store, or access your photos or videos on any external servers.
* **Location & Sensor Data:** Your GPS coordinates and hardware sensor telemetry (compass azimuth, magnetic field, atmospheric pressure) are processed in real time on your device to generate visual stamps.

## 3. Third-Party Services & APIs
When you use the app with an active internet connection, your approximate or precise coordinates (latitude and longitude) are sent to the following third-party services solely to retrieve stamp details (no personally identifiable information is attached):
* **Google Play Services (Fused Location Provider):** For acquiring accurate device location.
* **OpenStreetMap (Nominatim & Tile Servers), ArcGIS, OpenTopoMap, & CARTO:** To display mini-map previews and convert GPS coordinates into readable street addresses.
* **BigDataCloud & ipwho.is:** Used as fallback services for reverse geocoding and network-based location when GPS satellite signals are unavailable.
* **Open-Meteo API:** To fetch real-time local weather (temperature, wind speed, humidity, pressure) for your coordinates.

## 4. Data Sharing and Selling
We **do not** sell, trade, or rent your personal data or location history to third parties or advertisers.

## 5. User Control & Data Deletion
* You can revoke Camera, Microphone, or Location permissions at any time from your Android device's **Settings > Apps > GPS Map Camera > Permissions**.
* You can delete any captured photos or videos directly from the app's **Collection** screen or your phone's Gallery at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored app databases.

## 6. Children's Privacy
Our application does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
